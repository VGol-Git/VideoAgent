Grounded Video Agent
Local source: grounded-video-agent-main/grounded-video-agent-main/
License: Apache License, Version 2.0 (see the source project's LICENSE).
The weighted reciprocal-rank contribution and evidence-fusion approach in
clipscout/retrieval.py adapts capabilities/retrieval/candidate_fusion/capability.py.
RapidOCR conversion in clipscout/ocr.py adapts infrastructure/ocr/rapidocr_backend.py.
